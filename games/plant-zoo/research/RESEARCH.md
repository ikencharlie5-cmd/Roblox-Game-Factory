# Plant Zoo — R&D Research

## Summary
- **Plant Zoo 2nd Generation could not be found online.** About 30 searches across Roblox.com, Fandom, YouTube, TikTok, Reddit, rolimons/rotrends and code sites found nothing. Every engine maps "Plant Zoo 2" to Frontier's PC game *Planet Zoo 2*, and on TikTok "plant zoo" is mostly a misspelling of Planet Zoo. The game is probably small, very new, or titled differently on Roblox. **We need Charlie's link or screenshots.** The review below is based on Charlie's brief plus proven genre games, and every guess is labeled.
- **The proven genre formula (2025–26)** is: buy random-rarity items from a timed restock or conveyor, place them on a plot, they earn $/s even while you're offline, expand land, rebirth, and repeat. Grow a Garden (21M+ CCU peak), Steal a Brainrot (25.4M CCU) and Plants vs Brainrots all use it. Build a Zoo (Century Farmers, about 500M visits) is the closest "zoo" version.
- **Mutations and live weather events are the big hook.** In Grow a Garden, Gold (x20, 1%) and Rainbow (x50, 0.1%) mutations plus weather mutations (Wet x2 up to Celestial x120) create jackpot moments and reasons to log in during events.
- **Monetization that works:** 2x Money (about 800 R$), VIP (+25% income, 249 R$), a season pass (749 R$, 50 tiers, 6 daily quests), restock skips, and extra plot/pet slots. Players complain loudly about pay-to-win and stealing.
- **Our original twist:** plants that grow into creatures. You plant a seed, it grows through stages into a living "plant creature" (a mix of plant and animal) that goes in your zoo exhibit and draws visitors, who pay cash. That keeps the brief's plant + zoo mix without copying anyone.

### Core loop & first 5 minutes
- [BRIEF] Start with a small, empty zoo and a little cash. Buy seeds, plants and creatures, place them in the zoo, watch them grow, collect money from visitors and exhibits, find rarer species, expand, upgrade, rebirth, visit other zoos, and trade.
- [GENRE] The first 5 minutes in peer games:
  - **Grow a Garden:** an empty plot and a few coins buy carrot seeds; plant, harvest, sell, and buy better seeds. Crops keep growing offline.
  - **Build a Zoo:** a short tutorial (codes are locked until it's done), then buy eggs off a conveyor, place them on open squares, and they hatch into animals. You collect cash by walking near them.
  - **Steal a Brainrot:** buy units off a central conveyor and put them on pedestals to earn $/s.
- [GUESS] Likely first 5 minutes for Plant Zoo 2: a free starter seed, plant it in a pen, a 30–60 s first growth, the first visitor pays cash, buy a second seed from the shop, then the first land expansion around minute 3–5.

### Planting & growth stages / timers
- [BRIEF] Each plant has a growth timer, several stages, sell value, income value, rarity and a unique look. Watering and upgrades speed up growth.
- [GENRE] Grow a Garden crops grow offline. In Plants vs Brainrots, a Water Bucket speeds growth by 25% and each plot row holds 5 plants.
- [GUESS] 3–4 stages (seed, sprout, bloom, mature/creature). Timers scale with rarity: Common about 30 s, Legendary about 10 min, Secret about 1 h.

### Species / rarity tiers & odds
- [BRIEF] Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythical, Secret.
- [GENRE] Peer ladders:
  - **Build a Zoo:** Common to Uncommon to Rare to Epic to Legendary to Mythical, plus Divine and Prismatic (Prismatic eggs under 1%).
  - **Plants vs Brainrots:** Rare to Epic to Legendary to Mythic to Godly to Secret.
  - **Steal a Brainrot income bands:** Common $1–5/s, Rare $25–100/s, Epic $250–1k/s, Legendary $3k–10k/s, Mythic up to $50k/s, God $200k+/s. That is roughly ×10 per tier.

### Income / visitors
- [BRIEF] Money comes from visitors, plant production, exhibits, selling, daily rewards and quests.
- [GENRE] Build a Zoo animals earn passively, including offline, and cash is collected by walking near them. Build a Zoo-style guides also mention ticket prices and visitor satisfaction (low-trust source).
- [GUESS] Visitors walk a path, stop at exhibits, and pay based on exhibit value.

### Zoo/plot expansion & building
- [BRIEF] Place, move and rotate exhibits, plants, decor and paths. Expand land.
- [GENRE] Build a Zoo: each tile costs more than the last, and new sections unlock as the zoo grows. Guides recommend grid layouts.

### Upgrades
- [GENRE] Build a Zoo upgrades the conveyor through 8 tiers (Common to Prismatic), which raises egg quality and mutation chances. Plants vs Brainrots has a Gear Shop (Water Bucket and others).

### Mutations (likely present; genre standard)
- [GENRE] **Grow a Garden:** Gold x20 (1%), Rainbow x50 (0.1%), and weather mutations: Wet x2 (rain), Chilled x2, Sandy x3, Bloodlit x5, Acidic x15, Eclipsed x20, Shocked x100, Celestial x120 (meteor shower), Voidtouched x135.
- [GENRE] **Build a Zoo:** Gold (2x, from feeding a Pear), Diamond, Electric, Fire and Jurassic (up to about 12x). A meteor shower every 25 min lasting 5 min raises mutation odds.

### Rebirth
- [BRIEF] Permanent bonuses.
- [GENRE] **Plants vs Brainrots:** R1 costs $1M, R2 $50M, R3 $100M, R4 $150M, R5–6 $300M, R7 $1B, R8 $1.4B, R9–11 $2B. Each also needs 2 specific units. Each rebirth gives +50% money and +50% luck, a bigger island and a card pack. Cash resets; plants, gear and eggs are kept.
- [GENRE] **Steal a Brainrot:** rebirth takes about 8–10 h and about $100k/s. It wipes units and gives a multiplier, more pedestals and VIP zones.

### Trading / visiting
- [BRIEF] Trading and visiting other zoos.
- [GENRE] Grow a Garden has a large trading economy, but it also has real-money-trading and scam problems. Build a Zoo lets players steal from other zoos. Stealing is divisive.
- **Recommendation:** visit and "like" other zoos, plus trading with a two-sided confirm. No stealing.

### Events / codes / quests
- [GENRE] Grow a Garden runs weekly updates with timed weather and admin events, plus 6 daily season-pass quests worth 250 XP each. Build a Zoo runs a meteor shower every 25 min and seasonal events. Codes are usually redeemed through a gear/settings icon and a Codes tab.

### What players love / complain about / what YouTubers highlight
- **Love:** jackpot rare hatches and mutations, watching income climb while away, showing off bases, live events, and absurd or cute creatures.
- **Complain:** heavy monetization (Grow a Garden is criticized for it), stealing (losing a big purchase in a 3 s theft), scams and real-money trading, and "standard AFK game" depth (Kotaku, PCGamesN).
- **YouTube title patterns** (from search results): "Hatching & GROWING GIGANTIC Animals", "Building the Biggest Zoo", "100 Days building the BEST ZOO", and "Using 1000 seeds". Creators want **scale, gigantic sizes and big rare reveals.**

### Competitors — what they do better
- **Grow a Garden:** global restock timers and server-wide weather (shared moments), deep mutation stacking, offline growth, and a strong season pass.
- **Steal a Brainrot:** a conveyor in the middle of the map that everyone watches, with clear $/s tiers.
- **Plants vs Brainrots:** adds light action (plants fight waves). Clear rebirth goals that name the units you need.
- **Build a Zoo:** the closest theme. Conveyor tier upgrades, tile-by-tile expansion, and timed meteor events. Its creative building is its strength.

## For Ideas
- **Hook (first 60 s):** a free starter seed sprouts on screen within 15 s into a cute plant-creature, the first visitor walks up and pays, a coin pop sound plays, and an "Expand Zoo" button pulses.
- **Original twist ideas:** seeds grow into creatures. "Pollen weather" events mutate any growing plants. A hybrid exhibit where two species cross-pollinate into a new species (light breeding). Visitors' thought bubbles show which species they want.
- **Copy (mechanics are fine):** a timed seed restock (every 5 min) or seed conveyor, $/s per species, ×~10 income per tier, Gold/Rainbow-style growth mutations, server-wide weather every ~20–25 min, tile expansion with rising cost, rebirth with stated requirements, daily quests feeding a pass track, and offline earnings.
- **Do NOT copy:** names "Plant Zoo", "Grow a Garden", "Brainrot", "Build a Zoo"; species names; their logos, thumbnails, UI art or music; brainrot meme characters. Also skip mutation names that are signature to Grow a Garden ("Voidtouched", "Bloodlit"); make our own.
- **Avoid:** stealing (top complaint), a pay-to-win feel, and a long tutorial that blocks codes.

## For Gameplay
- **Rarity ladder (suggested):** Common 60%, Uncommon 25%, Rare 10%, Epic 4%, Legendary 0.9%, Mythical 0.09%, Secret 0.01% in the base shop. Upgrades and rebirth luck shift these odds.
- **Income:** use base $/s × 10^(tier index) roughly (Common 1–5, Rare 25–100, Legendary 3k–10k, based on Steal a Brainrot bands).
- **Mutations:** Gold ×20 at 1% and Rainbow ×50 at 0.1% are the Grow a Garden reference points. Weather mutations ×2 to ×100+. Multipliers add together.
- **Rebirth:** cost ladder roughly ×2–×50 per step (Plants vs Brainrots: $1M, then $50M, then $100M ...). Each rebirth gives +50% money. Keep the collection index and gear; reset cash and plot.
- **Offline growth:** save `os.time()` at planting, compute elapsed time on join, look up the stage from a table, and run a ~1 s Heartbeat countdown while online ([DevForum offline gardening](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/going-about-to-create-an-offline-gardening-system/391833)). Cap offline earnings (e.g. 8 h).
- **Placement:** use a grid snap with client preview and server validation, and save positions relative to the plot origin. References: [Grid placement thread](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-a-grid-placement-system/2723673), [Placement Service](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-use-placement-service/698753), [Furniture placement](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/creating-a-furniture-placement-system/205509), [Placement saving](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/placement-system-saving/334078).
- **Global restock:** use a shared seeded RNG from `os.time() // 300` so all servers show the same stock without MessagingService (idea from the [Grow a Garden tutorial series](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/how-to-make-roblox-grow-a-garden-game-tutorial-series/3769116) comments).
- **Trading:** two-sided offer, a 3 s countdown after both accept, and a server-side swap with a re-check of ownership.

## For Graphics
- Style: bright, low-poly, cartoony greenhouse/zoo. Rounded fences, wooden signs, glass domes, floating plots like sky-island tycoons.
- Palette suggestions:
  - Grass `#7BD148`, path `#E8D3A3`, fence wood `#A8673A`, water `#4FC3F7`, sky `#9EDCFF`
  - Rarity colors: Common `#B0B0B0`, Uncommon `#5BD35B`, Rare `#3BA7FF`, Epic `#A55BFF`, Legendary `#FFB300`, Mythical `#FF3D7F`, Secret = animated rainbow/black `#1A1A1A` with glow
- Growth stages: scale tween (0.3 → 1.0) with a bounce, and a sparkle burst at maturity. Gold mutation gets a gold material and shine; Rainbow gets a hue-cycling Highlight.
- Map: a central hub with a seed shop and sell/trade area, plus 6–8 plots around it in a ring. Each plot has a gate, a visitor path loop and expandable tiles. Weather events tint the lighting.
- Day/night cycle with warm sunset `#FF9E5E` and night `#1E2A4A`; fireflies at night.

## For UI
- Peers' common layout: currency top-center or top-left, a vertical button bar on the left (Shop, Inventory, Index, Build, Trade, Quests), and the event/weather banner top-center with a timer. Restock countdown in the shop. Big green "Buy" and red "Sell" buttons.
- Collection Index: a grid of silhouettes that reveal on discovery, colored by rarity, with a "x/y found" bar and a reward per page.
- Global announcement: "PLAYER found a SECRET ___!" banner plus a chat system message. A server-wide sound plays for Mythical and above.
- Mobile: use Scale sizes, a UIAspectRatioConstraint and large touch targets. Keep the right thumb area clear (Roblox jump button). See [Roblox UI/UX docs](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/game-design/ui-ux-design).
- Season pass screen: two-track Free/Premium layout with 6 daily quests (Grow a Garden model).

## For Audio
- Grow a Garden reference: calm classical/pastoral music for normal play (Grieg "Morning Mood", Vivaldi Spring), soft piano at night (Satie, Debussy), and energetic synth/rock/disco for events. **Do not reuse those exact arrangements**; pick our own Creator Store tracks in the same moods (search "pastoral", "cozy farm", "lofi garden", "upbeat event").
- Signature sounds: coin pop and ka-ching on collect, a sprout "bloop" on each growth stage, a rising chime and sparkle on maturity, a rarity-scaled fanfare (bigger for rarer), a weather sting (thunder/rain loop), a visitor chatter ambience and birds.
